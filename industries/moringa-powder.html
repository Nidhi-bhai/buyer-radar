Where to find buyers for Moringa powder

Importing countries

Moringa leaf powder is commonly declared under HS 121190 (bills of lading often show a national split such as 12119029). Comtrade's heading also covers other plants used in pharmacy, perfumery, or as insecticides. Country dollars below are for that whole heading.
